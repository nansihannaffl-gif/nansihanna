Who We Are

America's #1 Independent Life Insurance Agency

Family First Life (FFL) is a national insurance marketing organization dedicated to one mission: putting families first. We partner with the top-rated carriers in the country to offer clients the very best in life insurance, mortgage protection, and final expense coverage.

Founded on the belief that every family deserves financial security, FFL has grown into a powerhouse of over 35,000 licensed agents across the United States — all working to protect the people who matter most.

Final Expense Insurance

Mortgage Protection

Term life policies tailored specifically to pay off a client's mortgage if they pass away unexpectedly — ensuring their family keeps the home they built together, no matter what.

  • —Tied to your exact mortgage balance
  • —Return of premium options
  • —Disability & critical illness riders

Term & Whole Life
